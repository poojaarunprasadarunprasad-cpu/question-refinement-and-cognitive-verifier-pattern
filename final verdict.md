QUESTION REFINEMENT PATERN 

WHEN TO USE IT : Start with Question Refinement for Broad Work. When you are staring at a blank page for an essay or project, tell the AI to suggest a sharper version of your prompt. This helps you find a specific angle before you waste time reading generic summaries.

COGNITIVE VERIFIER PATTERN 

WHEN TO USE IT : Switch to Cognitive Verifier for Deep Study. Once you know what you are studying, ask the AI to break your main question into three to five smaller questions and answer them all. This forces the AI to double-check its logic and gives you a built-in study guide.
